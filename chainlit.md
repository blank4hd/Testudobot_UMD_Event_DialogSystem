# Welcome to TestudoBot! 🐢

**TestudoBot** is a conversational assistant that helps you discover University of Maryland events using natural language.

## What can I do?

- **Ask about events** — "Are there any career fairs this week?" or "What music events are coming up?"
- **Filter by topic** — Use the settings panel to filter results by category (e.g., Music, Career, Sports).
- **Try quick actions** — Click the buttons below for common queries like Free Food or Career Fairs.
- **Refresh data** — Type `/refresh` or click 🔄 Refresh Events to pull the latest events from the UMD calendar.

## Tips

- I understand relative dates like "this weekend", "tomorrow", or "next month".
- I can handle specific queries like "Is there free food on campus today?" or "Any hackathons in March?"
- My answers are grounded in real event data — I'll include source links so you can verify.
